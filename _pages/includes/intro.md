My research is driven by a fundamental interest in how AI can perceive, understand, and interact with the physical world. I pursue this vision through **advanced structural sensing, inspection, and monitoring**, with the long-term goal of developing **Generalizable Digital Twin Paradigms** for smarter, safer, and more resilient cities. I am particularly interested in visual measurement, multimodal information fusion, domain adaptation and generalization, zero- and few-shot learning, data stream mining, incremental learning, learning with noisy labels, and explainable AI (XAI).
